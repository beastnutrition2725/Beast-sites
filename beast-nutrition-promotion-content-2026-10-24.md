## Publishing checklist

- Replace every price placeholder with a confirmed current price.
- Confirm stock and exact packaging before publishing.
- Use the matching product image from the delivered asset ZIP.
- Add the website product URL to every post once the page is live.
- Use label-compliant descriptions; do not add medical, therapeutic, weight-loss, bodybuilding, hormone, or performance claims unless verified and legally approved.
- Confirm delivery timeframe and payment option for each order.
- Add a clear returns and customer-support policy to the website.
- Keep the catalog note that images are for representation and availability/prices may change.
