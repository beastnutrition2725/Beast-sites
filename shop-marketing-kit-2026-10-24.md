### Working brand profile
- Shop name: [SHOP NAME]

- Ideal customer: [TARGET CUSTOMER]
- Service area: [CITY / REGION / ONLINE]
- Main conversion goal: [SHOP ONLINE / BOOK / MESSAGE / VISIT STORE]
- Primary call to action: Shop the collection

### Positioning statement
[SHOP NAME] helps [IDEAL CUSTOMER] find [PRIMARY BENEFIT] through [PRODUCTS / SERVICE], with a focus on [QUALITY, STYLE, CONVENIENCE, CRAFT, VALUE, OR OTHER VERIFIED DIFFERENTIATOR].

### Brand promise
A confident, simple shopping experience built around products you can feel good about choosing.

### Voice
Professional, warm, clear, confident, useful, and customer-focused. Avoid exaggerated claims, pressure tactics, slang that weakens trust, and unsupported superlatives.

### Message pillars
1. Product value: Explain what the product does, who it suits, and why it is useful.
2. Trust: Use real materials, sourcing, guarantees, reviews, delivery information, and customer care details.
3. Experience: Make browsing, choosing, ordering, and receiving the product feel easy.
4. Proof: Show real product photography, demonstrations, customer feedback, and behind-the-scenes work.

### Visual direction
Use clean product-led photography, consistent lighting, uncluttered backgrounds, generous white space, and one recognizable accent color. Recommended working palette until brand colors are supplied: ink #18212B, warm white #F7F5F0, accent #C47B52, muted sage #87968A. Use one display font with a highly readable sans-serif body font; keep contrast accessible and text legible on mobile.

## 7. Publishing standards

Before publishing, confirm every bracketed field is replaced or removed; product names, prices, delivery times, stock, discounts, reviews, guarantees, claims, and statistics are accurate; every link works; images have descriptive alt text; captions are readable without audio; logos and colors are consistent; and the checkout, returns, privacy, and contact pages are live.

Recommended image exports: website hero 1920×900, website product image 1600×1600, Instagram square 1080×1080, Instagram portrait 1080×1350, story/reel 1080×1920, Facebook link image 1200×630, LinkedIn image 1200×627, Pinterest pin 1000×1500. Export as WebP or optimized JPG where supported, and keep an original high-resolution master.

## 8. Measurement plan

Track website sessions from each platform with UTM links. Review reach, saves, clicks, product views, add-to-cart actions, checkout starts, purchases or enquiries, and cost per result where advertising is used. Keep the message that produces qualified action; replace weak hooks and creative rather than changing several variables at once.
