## THUMBNAIL TEMPLATE SPECS

### Design Rules
- **Text:** MAX 5 words, bold impact font (Bebas Neue, Impact, or anime-style)
- **Colors:** High contrast — dark background with bright accent (red/orange)
- **Face:** Close-up character face is mandatory — eye contact with viewer
- **Arrows/Circles:** Use to highlight key element (muscle, weight, transformation)

### Top-Performing Thumbnail Formulas
1. **The Shock Pose:** Character mid-transformation + big text + fire/explosion effects
2. **The Before/After:** Split image showing weak → strong transformation
3. **The Face-Off:** Two characters about to fight + YOU in the middle
4. **The Impossible Lift:** Character holding insane weight with reaction shots around
